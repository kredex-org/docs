## Future Mainnet Enhancements

Based on ongoing feedback, the following features are planned for our Mainnet release:
- **Fiat On/Off Ramps:** Integration with MoneyGram Access for seamless cash-to-crypto onboarding.
- **Stablecoin Integration:** Defaulting loans to USDC (as requested by users) to mitigate XLM price volatility.
- **Third-Party Identity Verification:** Upgrading from mock SEP-12 to real-world biometrics via Sumsub/Jumio.
- **Decentralized Oracles:** Real-time interest rate adjustments based on global macro-economic data feeds via Soroban oracles.
