# Walkthrough & Usage

Kredex is designed to be simple for both borrowers and lenders. Here is the typical workflow for each type of user.

## For Borrowers

1. **Sign Up & KYC:**
   - Log in seamlessly with your **Freighter Wallet** or use passwordless **Passkeys**.
   - Navigate to the **KYC Verify** page and complete your SEP-12 identity verification.
2. **Build Reputation:**
   - Your on-chain reputation score starts at a baseline level (e.g., 250).
   - This score determines your borrowing limit.
3. **Request a Loan:**
   - Go to **Apply for Loan**.
   - Enter your requested amount (up to your limit) and duration.
   - Wait for a lender to fund your loan from the marketplace or for it to be fulfilled by a lending pool.
4. **Repay & Earn Trust:**
   - Once your loan is due, navigate to **Repay Loan**.
   - Sign the Soroban transaction to return the principal plus interest.
   - A successful repayment boosts your on-chain reputation score, unlocking higher borrowing limits for your next loan!

## For Lenders

1. **Sign Up & Connect Wallet:**
   - Log in and connect your **Freighter Wallet** (Stellar Testnet).
2. **Explore the Marketplace:**
   - Go to the **Loan Marketplace**.
   - Browse open loan requests and view borrower metrics (Reputation Score, KYC Status, Requested Amount).
3. **Fund a Loan:**
   - Click **Fund Loan** on any request you like.
   - Sign the Soroban escrow transaction. Your funds are locked securely until the borrower repays or defaults.
4. **Pool Investment (Passive Income):**
   - Don't want to pick loans manually? Go to **Pool Investment**.
   - Deposit XLM into the automated lending pool. The pool automatically funds high-reputation borrowers and shares the yield with depositors.
5. **Monitor Your Portfolio:**
   - Check the **Portfolio** and **History** tabs to track your active loans, earned interest, and upcoming repayments.
